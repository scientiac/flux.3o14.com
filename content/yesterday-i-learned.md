### 3. There exists cells extracted from a human that hasn't died since 1951.
Source: [Michael Discovered A New Way To Make Twins](https://youtu.be/KBKpuJttC3Y?si=8tEpW4RYHMc8Up2P)

There is an immortalized cell line used in scientific research called HeLa. These cells are durable and prolific, allowing for extensive applications in scientific study and medical research. It is derived from cervical cancer cells taken on February 8, 1951, from Henrietta Lacks, a 31-year-old African-American woman, after whom the line is named. Lacks died of cancer on October 4, 1951.
