[_What do you do when you find your family doing something very wrong, but they have so much power over you that you cannot do anything about it?_]

### Rules
1. You cannot speak up.
1. There is no changing their behaviour.
1. There is no one else who can convince them.

### Options
1. Run away.
1. Stop Interacting.
1. Keep calm and ignore.
1. Close your eyes.
1. Make plans for leaving and never returning.
1. Cry about it.
1. Work hard, get rich, be independent and help the ones who can be affected by them. [_(But, you are dependent on them at them mean time while you are planning this and this will take time.)_]
1. Just look at the things that are happening, be quiet and slowly lose your mind like everyone else has done till now.
