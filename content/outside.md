Far from the comfort of my four walls.   
I'm sitting on an uncomfortable steel bench.  
No people, no phones, it's green all around.  
It's a weirdly comforting place that I've found.   

I can hear the leaves falling from above.  
I can see the birds chirping their songs.  
Monkeys swinging and the crickets creaking.  
In this beauty of chaos, peace, is what I'm feeling.  

It's been hours. My sweat has all dried up.  
My clothes are dry and I'm not tired.  
My legs well rested, breath it's calm.  
This place is cool. I crave something warm.   

I need to return, get back to my work.  
To those four walls. Now I cannot stall.  
There, I'll stop running away and quit quitting.  
So I can return here, to where I'm sitting.  
So I can sit here, without hearing this heart beating.  
So I can enjoy this, free from the secrets I'm keeping.
